# AI Review

AI Review helps you check whether your investment rule is complete.

## What AI Review can do

- identify missing fields
- suggest follow-up questions
- summarize your rule
- point out unclear assumptions
- check consistency

## What AI Review cannot do

- recommend buying
- recommend selling
- guarantee profit
- guarantee loss avoidance
- predict future stock price
- manage your portfolio

## If AI output is blocked

The app may block AI output if it looks like investment advice or a buy/sell recommendation.

In that case, revise the input or try again.
